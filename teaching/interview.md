# Interview and calibration

Read the learner profile and session first. Ask only what changes the next exercise: a real work problem or goal, recent experience, available session time, and a recent difficulty. Use conversation rather than a long questionnaire. Accept corrections and do not turn self-report into demonstrated competence.

Save goals, preferences, time budget, interaction language, and self-reported experience through `state apply`. Leave unknown fields empty/null. Summaries are English; preserve original answers separately when they are useful evidence.

Choose one small calibration task after the interview. Match its scope to the learner's time and prerequisites. Do not assign the framework fixture or automatically choose a queue/concurrency curriculum. Tell the learner what the exercise is intended to reveal. Transition onboarding → planning, record `next_action`, and follow `generate.md`.

Calibration is a learning opportunity, not a rank. Ask the learner to implement, predict, or explain something concrete; use that evidence to adjust the next task.
